Key Tips for Success
💧
Hydration & Nutrition
Start hydrating the night before long runs. Carry a bottle for runs over 75 min. Try a banana or date 30 min before. Practice your race-day fuel strategy during long runs — don't try anything new on Oct 18.
😴
Sleep is Training
7–8 hours of sleep matters as much as the run itself. Your body adapts during rest, not during the run. Prioritize sleep over early-morning workouts if you've had under 6 hrs.
🦵
Strength & Mobility
Add 15 min of glute bridges, calf raises, and hip flexor stretches on easy days. Strong glutes = injury prevention. A simple post-run stretch routine prevents tightness from compounding.
📲
HDOR App
VDHM is a virtual event — download the HDOR app and log your 21.1 km run on Oct 18. Use your registered email to access your race profile. Track your GPS to get your official finish time.
📅
Trust the Taper
Weeks 11–12 you'll feel sluggish and itchy to run more — don't. Taper preserves all your hard-earned fitness. Your legs need to arrive at the start line fresh, not just fit.
🎯
Race Strategy
Start at 7:00 min/km for the first 5 km (easier than target). Settle into 6:45 pace from km 5–18. Push the final 3 km. Going out too fast is the #1 mistake in half marathons.
